The identity

The W and the comet. Discipline and trajectory.

The monogram is built from the particle that carries the whole project — “w/”, with. The W stays strictly between its guide lines: the operator, the discipline. The golden comet launches from the very foot of the letter and breaks past the cap line: the trajectory. Its head is an octagon — a cut gem, a bolt head. The builder's comet.

03Construction

The W — flat-cut apex sitting exactly on the cap line, mitre-sharp spikes below the baseline. Butt-cut terminals, no rounding anywhere: engraved, not printed.

The comet — a quill-tapered trail that starts as a needle point at the lowest tip of the W's spike, widens as it climbs, and ends in an octagonal head floating above the cap line — the dot of an “i” that left the page. It reads as a ramp-up curve: the builder's trajectory.

One rule above all — the W never leaves its lines; only the comet does.
