Twelve years of operating real businesses — not watching from the sidelines.

Banking, hospitality, maritime logistics: three different industries, one constant — taking responsibility for the numbers, the teams, and the customers.

  • 2014–2016Corporate banking — Société Générale group, overseas branch.Management control and corporate cash management: budget control, financial reporting, decision tools — supporting SMEs and large accounts from inside their numbers.

A decade of P&Ls, payroll, suppliers and customers — hands-on and real. That is the foundation this project is built on.
